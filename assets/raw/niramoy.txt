Hackathon Strategic Positioning (Sector 001 — Primary Health Care): The core objective of Sector 001 is to ensure first-contact care, clinical screening, and Continuity of Care for citizens. Niramoy fulfills this mandate while directly satisfying all four judging assessment lenses through HL7 FHIR interoperability, hybrid queue tracking, and Zero-PII data protection.

1. Executive Summary
Healthcare access in Bangladesh remains fragmented and uncoordinated. Most patients struggle to determine which medical specialist aligns with their physical symptoms, frequently leading them to consult the wrong department or engage in unsafe self-medication at local pharmacies. Female patients in particular often experience delayed care due to difficulty locating verified female specialists nearby.
Niramoy is a locally contextualized digital primary health concierge and interoperable triage platform. Rather than issuing final prescriptions or diagnosing diseases, the platform uses an AI triage engine capable of understanding both Bangla and English to identify the appropriate medical department and generate a one-click, HL7 FHIR-compliant QR/PDF triage summary. It also integrates a Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council (BM&DC) registered doctor and hospital directory, a manual serial and hybrid queue tracker, and a verified diagnostic outcome review system.
2. Problem Statement
Incorrect Specialist Selection & Self-Medication: Patients who cannot accurately interpret their symptoms often visit the wrong clinical department or take unnecessary medications recommended by pharmacy attendants.
Lack of Continuity of Care & Interoperability: When patients transition between clinics or physicians, prior symptom histories and preliminary records are not carried in a standardized format, causing redundant diagnostic tests.
Deficit in Verified Chamber & Credential Information: Accurate chamber schedules and reliable phone numbers for manual serial booking are scarce, leaving patients confused and vulnerable to informal brokers.
Lack of Outcome Transparency & Trust: No reliable platform exists for patients to verify how a physician has previously managed similar cases or what patient recovery outcomes looked like.
Social & Gender-Based Barriers: Many female patients delay essential medical care due to limited availability or poor discoverability of female gynecologists, sonologists, and general practitioners.
3. Technical Framework & Interoperability (Tech Stack & Standards)
Frontend: Next.js optimized for fast loading over weak 3G/4G networks, mobile-responsive Tailwind CSS, and OpenStreetMap with Leaflet for interactive map rendering.
Backend: Python (FastAPI) or Node.js for low-latency data processing, review verification, and geo-location queries.
Database & Geo-Location: PostgreSQL paired with the PostGIS extension for mapping Dhaka's thanas and calculating patient-to-chamber distances.
AI & Clinical Standards: Gemini API operated under strict system prompts and zero temperature to perform organ-system triage (without disease diagnosis) and map outputs to HL7 FHIR R4 (Condition, ReferralRequest) and ICD-10 symptom codes (R-codes).
Security & Notification Channels: Zero-PII triage architecture, AES-256 encryption, and a local SMS gateway for dispatching emergency helpline alerts and live queue tokens.
4. Core Platform Features
4.1 AI Triage, Specialist Routing & FHIR QR Triage Summary
The system analyzes natural symptom descriptions in both Bangla and English (for example, "I have a headache and feel nauseous" or "মাথা ব্যথা আর বমি ভাব হচ্ছে"). It never prescribes medication or names diseases; instead, it offers basic non-pharmacological first aid (rest, oral rehydration/saline) and determines the right medical specialty. Upon completion, the patient can download an HL7 FHIR-compliant QR and PDF summary in one click.
Four-Step Organ-System Doctor Referral Workflow (Non-Diagnostic):
1. Red-Flag Emergency Gate: High-risk symptoms (chest pressure, shortness of breath, severe bleeding) immediately bypass AI triage and route the user to national emergency services (999 / 16263).
2. Organ-System Detection from Symptoms: Rather than searching for disease names, the AI identifies the affected physiological system (e.g., cardiovascular/respiratory, nervous, ENT, gastrointestinal, musculoskeletal), along with duration and severity.
3. Follow-Up Questions & Safe Default: When symptoms overlap multiple systems (e.g., "abdominal pain"), the engine asks 1–2 brief clarifying questions; for vague or multi-system presentations, it safely defaults to Internal Medicine / General Practitioner (GP).
4. Organ System → Specialty Tag → Doctor List Referral: The detected organ system maps to a Specialty Tag (e.g., Nervous System → Neurology; Musculoskeletal → Orthopedics). The backend queries PostgreSQL for BM&DC-verified doctors (filtered by area, gender, and outcome reviews) and outputs an FHIR summary with ICD-10 R-codes.
4.2 Verified Doctor & Hospital Directory
Every physician profile displays a verified BM&DC registration number, approved degrees (MBBS, FCPS, MD), chamber address, and consultation fee. Patients can filter results by physician gender, neighborhood (e.g., Dhanmondi, Uttara, Mirpur), and travel distance.
4.3 Chamber Schedule, Manual Serial & Hybrid Smart Queue Tracker
Existing direct phone numbers for manual serial booking at traditional chambers remain intact. Alongside them, a lightweight one-screen Chamber Assistant Portal and SMS queue token system enable assistants to update live serial numbers so patients can track queue progress on their phones and retain verified proof of visit completion.
4.4 Patient Outcome & Clinical Approach Review Platform
After a consultation, patients can submit structured feedback on diagnostic clarity, clinical approach, and recovery outcomes. To prevent fake reviews, submissions are verified via prescription slip OCR and phone OTP, and prescription images are automatically deleted from the server immediately after verification.
4.5 Emergency Helpline & Ambulance Connect
When severe symptoms are detected, the platform bypasses standard triage to display a quick-dial screen for National Emergency Service (999) and Shastho Batayon (16263), paired with a Leaflet map highlighting nearby Emergency Rooms (ER) and an ambulance directory.

8. Future Work & Specialized Healthcare Expansion
Following the Dhaka primary-care pilot, Synity Soft will expand Niramoy into a modular, interoperable healthcare ecosystem supporting specialized clinical verticals while retaining strict Bangla and English accessibility and HL7 FHIR standards.
8.1 Smart Geo-Qualified Routing (GPS & Outcome-Ranked Referral)
Currently, patients search for doctors by selecting an area or thana (e.g., Dhanmondi, Uttara). In future releases, with explicit patient consent, real-time GPS location and PostGIS geo-queries will automatically rank and recommend the highest-qualified specialists nearby—combining BM&DC credentials, clinical experience, and verified patient outcome review scores.
8.2 Maternity & Maternal-Child Care Module
A dedicated maternal health vertical will support expectant mothers through trimester-aware Bangla and English symptom triage, automated red-flag escalation for obstetric emergencies (such as preeclampsia warning signs or bleeding), priority discovery of verified female OB/GYNs and sonologists, SMS reminders for antenatal/postnatal checkups, and portable HL7 FHIR maternal care summaries.

10. National Impact, Public Service Intention & Ethical Commitment
Synity Soft is building Niramoy first and foremost as a public-interest digital health utility. Our moral commitment is to protect vulnerable patients from misinformation, exploitation, and privacy harm while elevating trust across Bangladesh's healthcare system.
Unbiased, Non-Commercial Triage Integrity: AI specialist routing and organic search results remain strictly neutral and are never influenced by sponsored hospital listings or commercial incentives.
Patient Safety & Zero-PII Dignity: Strict non-diagnostic guardrails prevent unsafe AI prescriptions, immediate 999/16263 escalation protects emergencies, and Zero-PII triage safeguards patient privacy.
Curbing Self-Medication & Broker Exploitation: Directing citizens to verified BM&DC doctors and transparent chamber serials reduces harmful pharmacy self-medication, antibiotic misuse, and middleman extortion.
Equitable National Health Impact: Empowering female patients to locate verified female specialists and expanding into maternity and chronic care will lower redundant diagnostic costs and strengthen primary care continuity nationwide.
